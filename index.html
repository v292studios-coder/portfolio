Vishnu Kumar - 292 STUDIOS

I'm Vishnu Kumar, a Portrait Photographer based in Sayreville, NJ. From a young age, I've always been a creative. From getting chastised for drawing in the margins of my assignments in middle school to taking the creative visions in my mind and bringing them to life with my camera. I've got a great eye for details and pursuing a creative vision.

I've had the pleasure of providing high-quality professional images for local creatives in the NJ dance and automotive communities. I love working with creative individuals who desire to create content that is not just seen, but felt.

If you want to create photos that stand out and tell a story, let's work together.
