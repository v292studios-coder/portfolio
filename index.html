THE PHOTOGRAPHER

I am Vishnu Kumar, a portrait and lifestyle photographer based in Sayreville, NJ. Operating a home studio, I specialize in client headshots, creative concept shoots, and intimate lifestyle sessions — bringing out authenticity in every frame.

Every session is a collaboration. I work closely with each client to creatively direct the shoot from styling and mood through to the final edit, delivered via Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop.

EQUIPMENT & WORKFLOW

  • Studio: Home studio, Sayreville NJ — controlled lighting environment
  • Specialties: Headshots, creative concept shoots, lifestyle photography
  • Post-processing: Adobe Lightroom & Photoshop (Creative Cloud)
  • Delivery: High-resolution digital exports, archival backups
