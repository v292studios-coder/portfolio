PORTFOLIO

A showcase of portrait, event, and creative lifestyle photography projects.

Currently, I focus primarily on high-school and college graduation portraiture. In the near future, I plan to expand into larger creative projects, incorporating both photography and videography into 292 STUDIOS.

Vishnu Kumar - 292 STUDIOS

I am Vishnu Kumar, a portrait photographer based in Sayreville, NJ. From a young age, I have always been a creative—from getting chastised for drawing in the margins of my middle school assignments, to now taking the visions in my mind and bringing them to life through my camera lens. I have a keen eye for detail and a relentless drive to pursue a creative vision. I am so passionate about this craft that I have spent the last four years building and funding my own studio, ensuring I have the perfect environment to capture the true essence of every photoshoot.

I have also been incredibly blessed to be an active part of the NJ dance community for over five years. Through this connection, I have had the absolute pleasure of providing high-quality, professional imagery for my fellow artists. I love collaborating with creative individuals who desire to produce content that is not just seen, but felt.

If you are looking to have more than just your likeness captured in a photo, let's work together to create something that can move the masses.

EQUIPMENT & WORKFLOW

  • Studio: Home studio, Sayreville NJ — controlled lighting environment
  • Specialties: Headshots, creative concept shoots, lifestyle photography
  • Post-processing: Adobe Lightroom & Photoshop (Creative Cloud)
  • Delivery: High-resolution digital exports, archival backups
